DeskCYD

A desk controller for your Cheap Yellow Display · version __VERSION__

1 Install on the CYD

Plug the CYD into this computer with USB, then click Install and pick its port. Works in Chrome or Edge.

This browser can't install. Open this page in Chrome or Edge. Open this page over https to install.

No port shows up? Install the CH340 USB driver and try again. Stuck on "connecting"? Hold the BOOT button on the back of the CYD.

2 Connect it to Wi-Fi

The CYD restarts and shows Wi-Fi setup steps. On your phone, join the DeskCYD-Setup Wi-Fi, tap Configure WiFi, choose your home network and enter its password. You only do this once.

3 Run the PC app

Download DeskCYD.exe

  1. Double-click it. If Windows says it protected your PC, click More info → Run anyway.
  2. When the firewall asks, allow it on Private networks.
  3. It sits in the system tray and starts with Windows. Right-click the icon to change that or quit.

Play something on your PC and it shows up on the CYD. Your PC and the CYD need to be on the same Wi-Fi.
